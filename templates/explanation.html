How does this game work?

The goal is to create a funny story by filling in the blank words.
  1. One player starts the game and sends it to the others.
  2. Every player fills in a section of the story. For example, if the blank is "a man's name", the player enters Horace
  3. Once all the blanks have been filled in, the story is complete and everyome gets to see it.

Here's an example story where all the blanks have been filled in:

Mediocre Joe met transparent Kim at the bowling alley. Joe wore a seafoam green leisure suit. Kim wore a sandwich board. Joe said to Kim “During the last storm, we had a little party in the mud.” Kim said “She wasn’t that into me.” As a consequence, the band got back together. And the world said “Somehow, I think I saw this coming
